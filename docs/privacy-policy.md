## What we collect

| Data | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address, password (stored hashed by our database provider) | To create and secure your account | You. Your name is visible to members of your club. |
| Club membership, team and role | To show the right fixtures and permissions | Members of your club |
| Availability for fixtures and any reason you give | So your captain can pick a team | Members of your club |
| Injury status (description, expected return, notes) | So your captain knows if you can play. **This is health data.** We process it only with your explicit consent, given when you enter it, and you can remove it at any time. | Members of your club |
| Match results and the form score calculated from them | Team selection and statistics | Members of your club |
| Partner feedback (ratings and comments) | To help partners improve | You, the partner who wrote it, your captain and club admins |
| Push notification token | To send you team notifications | Only us |

We do not collect your date of birth, location, contacts or advertising identifiers. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication) in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1). Push notifications are delivered via Expo, Apple and Google.

## Children

You must be 13 or over to create an account. A parent or guardian may create and manage an account for a younger player. Clubs are responsible for their own safeguarding policies.

## Your rights

You can:

- **See and correct** your profile in the app (Me → Profile).
- **Remove injury information** at any time (Me → Fitness → I've recovered).
- **Leave a club** (Me → Leave club).
- **Delete your account** in the app (Me → Delete my account). This permanently deletes your profile, availability, injury history, match participation and feedback.
- **Ask for a copy of your data or object to processing:** email [support email]. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

## Retention

We keep account data while your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account. Clubs' fixtures and team results remain for the club's records without your personal details.
